## Selection standard

Target a mixed US adult group. A hard prompt should be an enjoyable challenge involving a recognizable reference, rather than depending on a minor character name, specialist vocabulary, or a fleeting microtrend. Familiarity decisions are editorial judgments informed by the existing bank, the user’s calibration, and current sources; rankings alone do not establish familiarity. No addition quota was used.
